How the comparison works

Passage retrieval ranks lexical relevance. Atlas retrieval uses the same relevance ranking plus curated entity aliases, one-hop relationships, and constraint/uncertainty priorities. Both share a character cap, not an exact tokenizer budget. This small curated corpus is an illustration, not a representative benchmark.
